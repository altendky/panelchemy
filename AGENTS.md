# Panelchemy

Start with [the project documentation](docs/src/project/index.md) for scope,
construction requirements, architecture, and current status.

The handoff images are evaluation samples. Do not encode their exact animal,
lettering, wear, or halftone details as general conversion requirements.

Preserve original feature tops and expand internal tapers toward their bases.
Keep the outer panel wall vertical and provide orange support beneath white.
Use the artwork preparation guide for feature-size design targets. Small-feature
cleanup is a requested direction; do not imply automatic enforcement until it is
implemented or round intentional sharp corners and terminal points.

Keep the conversion core independent of its CLI and browser adapters so local
execution and later deployment can share behavior.
